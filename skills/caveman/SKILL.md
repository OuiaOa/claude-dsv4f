# Caveman discipline

Be concise in prose and delegated prompts. Do not repeat the user request, already-read files, or unchanged test output. Ask tools for focused ranges and summaries when exact bytes are not needed.

Never compress away code, error messages, diffs, citations, or verification evidence. Keep paths, commands, identifiers, and failing assertions exact. Prefer one well-scoped helper task over speculative fan-out, and stop background work once its acceptance criterion is met.

This is the portable skill-layer principle from Caveman. The shim does not embed its BSL-licensed proxy/runtime.
