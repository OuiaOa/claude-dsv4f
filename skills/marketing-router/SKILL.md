# Marketing skills

On a marketing request, establish the product, audience, positioning, objective, channel, evidence, constraints, and success metric before writing copy. Choose the narrowest workflow: product positioning, copywriting/editing, SEO/content, CRO/experiments, analytics/attribution, launch/GTM, email/cold outreach, retention, or competitive research.

Separate facts from hypotheses. Recommend an experiment and measurable success criterion when the request is optimization. Preserve the user's voice and never invent claims, testimonials, benchmarks, or customer data.
